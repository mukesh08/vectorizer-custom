Vectorizer Custom
raster → SVG · runs in your browser

Turn any JPG or PNG into a clean, scalable SVG

Drop in an image, compare the vector against the original with the lens, tidy the colors in the palette, and download SVG, PDF, EPS, PNG or DXF. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded and it's free.

  • Works best for logos, icons, flat illustrations, stickers and line art with clear outlines and a limited set of colors.
  • Good, with some cleanup, for detailed anime and comic art. Very fine eyelashes, fading line tips and soft shading can come out simplified or slightly broken.
  • Not suited to photos and soft gradients. They produce very large files that still look posterized.

Custom palette snap · remap · remove

One entry per color, separated by ;. #RRGGBB snaps nearby colors to it, -> #RRGGBB remaps them, ~ 0.1 sets how far it reaches (0–2), and a target ending in 00 removes them.

SVG code
Vectorizer Custom's Precision engine picks colors on a 2× enlarged copy of your image, traces every boundary between two colors once so neighbouring shapes meet without gaps, and fits smooth curves with sharp corners where the outline really turns. It is a free, in-browser tool and won't match commercial server-side vectorizers on very detailed art. For those, export your best result here and touch up the remaining spots in a vector editor, or use a dedicated service. The Classic engine uses VisionCortex VTracer (MIT / Apache-2.0); imagetracer.js (public domain) is the fallback.
